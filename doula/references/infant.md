# Infant (3 to 12 months)

## Starting solids
Around 6 months, when baby can sit with support, has lost the tongue thrust reflex, and shows interest in food. Iron rich first foods matter (fortified cereal, pureed meat, legumes). Any approach, purees or baby led weaning, is fine, follow her lead on what she's chosen. Introduce one new food at a time when watching for allergic reaction, a few days apart.

## Sleep
Regressions are common around 4mo, 8 to 10mo, and 12mo, usually tied to a developmental leap, not a step backward in her parenting. Naps consolidate from 3 down to 2 over this window. This is when many families consider gentle sleep training if they want to, entirely her call, never push one method as correct.

## Milestones, as ranges not deadlines
Rolling (around 4 to 6mo), sitting unsupported (around 6 to 8mo), crawling (around 7 to 10mo, some babies skip it entirely), pulling to stand (around 8 to 10mo), first words (around 9 to 14mo), first steps (around 9 to 15mo). Wide ranges are normal. Frame any gap as "worth mentioning at the next checkup," never as delay or diagnosis.

## Teething
Can start as early as 3mo or as late as 12mo. Drooling, chewing on everything, mild fussiness, slightly disrupted sleep. Never suggest a teething medication, gel, or specific remedy by name, that's a pediatrician or pharmacist question.

## Other common threads
Separation anxiety emerging (often 6 to 9mo, a sign of healthy attachment, not a problem to fix), stranger anxiety, babyproofing once mobility starts, growth spurts causing sudden appetite changes.

## Rules
- Read `red-flags.md` for fever, allergic reaction during food introduction, prolonged vomiting or diarrhea, or any loss of a previously reached milestone.
- Frame milestone questions gently. Ranges and reassurance first, "mention it at the next checkup" if she's genuinely worried, never a verdict.
