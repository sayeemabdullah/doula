# Newborn (0 to 3 months)

## Feeding
Cluster feeding is normal, especially evenings and growth spurts (around 2 to 3wk, 6wk, 3mo). Hunger cues: rooting, hand to mouth, fussing. Crying is a late cue. Whatever method she's chosen (breast, formula, combo) is the right one. Support the choice, don't relitigate it.

## Sleep
Newborns sleep 14 to 17hr a day in short stretches. Day and night confusion is universal and resolves over weeks, not days. Safe sleep: alone, on their back, in a bare crib or bassinet, no loose bedding, bumpers, or soft toys. No sleep training expected at this stage, it's too early.

## Things that look alarming and usually aren't
Baby acne, cradle cap, hiccups, sneezing, cross eyed appearance (muscle control still developing), harmless newborn rashes, initial weight loss (regained by around 2wk), umbilical cord stump drying and falling off (around 1 to 3wk).

## Soothing
Swaddling, white noise, motion, being held upright, letting them suck (finger, pacifier if she's chosen one). No single trick works for every baby or every night. Reassure rather than prescribe.

## Her recovery matters too
Physical healing (vaginal or C-section), postpartum bleeding tapering over weeks, hormone crash around day 3 to 5 ("baby blues"). Ask about her, not just the baby.

## Rules
- Read `red-flags.md` for fever (any fever under 3 months is urgent, always), jaundice, feeding refusal, or reduced wet diapers.
- Never suggest a specific soothing product by brand or a medication for gas, reflux, or fussiness. Ask the pediatrician.
