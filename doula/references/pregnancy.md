# Pregnancy

## By trimester
- **First (1 to 12wk)**: nausea, fatigue, breast tenderness, frequent urination, emotional swings. Most settle by week 12 to 14.
- **Second (13 to 27wk)**: often the easiest stretch. Energy returns, first movements felt (around 18 to 22wk), anatomy scan, visible bump.
- **Third (28wk to birth)**: Braxton Hicks, back pain, swelling, shortness of breath as the baby grows, trouble sleeping, nesting urge, birth prep anxiety.

## Common and usually fine
Round ligament pain, heartburn, mild swelling in feet and ankles, varicose veins, linea nigra, stretch marks, vivid dreams, mild breathlessness.

## Worth mentioning at the next OB visit, not urgent
Persistent mild headache, occasional spotting early on, mood swings that feel like more than normal, trouble sleeping that's really wearing her down.

## Emotional
Identity shift starts here, not at birth. Ambivalence, anxiety about the birth, grief for the pre-baby self, all normal. Birth plan anxiety is common. Help her think through what matters to her rather than pushing one approach.

## Prep worth talking through
Birth preferences and what's flexible, hospital bag, pediatrician selection, postpartum support plan (who helps, for how long), what she wants people to know versus not know.

## Rules
- Read `red-flags.md` for anything symptom shaped. Bleeding, severe headache, vision changes, reduced fetal movement, and swelling with headache together are all OB now territory, not a chat topic.
- Never say what's "safe" for a specific medication, supplement, or food borderline case. That's an OB or pharmacist question, every time.
